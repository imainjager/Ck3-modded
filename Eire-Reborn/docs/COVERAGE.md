# What Eire Reborn has touched, and what it has not (v0.13.2, checked against the installed game)

The game has about 120 content folders under `common/`, 70 event folders and 20 DLCs. The mod has 18 folders under `common/` (plus events, localization, history and a few images).

## Touched
Decisions (173), character events (383, in 27 files), buildings (regional, duchy, special and final), holdings lists, men-at-arms (Gallowglass, Fianna, Kern, Tara Guard, Insular Spears, Norse-Bane Axemen, Forest Warriors), culture traditions (10), tenets (15, personal and faith), landed titles (provincial kingdoms, Dal Riata, Gaeldom), coat of arms for those titles, nicknames, opinion modifiers, traits (fame traits), character, county, province, dynasty and artifact modifiers, scripted effects and triggers, on_actions (yearly pulses, war results), history (special building slots), invasions (Black Host, five Conquerors, Great Saxon Uprising) built from events, spawned armies and claim wars, artifacts, a legend, culture conversion pulses.

## Not touched at all
**Activities** (24 vanilla types): feast, hunt, festival, tournament, pilgrimage, wedding, funeral, coronation, debate, tour, camp party, university visit, gruesome festival, witch ritual, hike, chariot race. Nothing Irish: an Óenach (fair), Feis of Tara, Samhain/Beltane festival, cattle raid, a bardic contest, a fosterage gathering, a pilgrimage to Skellig/Croagh Patrick/Lough Derg, a hosting, a cain-law assembly.
**Cultures**: no new cultures, pillars (heritage, language, ethos, martial tradition), innovations, name lists, eras, aesthetic bundles, creation names, or name equivalency. Irish, Scottish, Welsh, Cornish, Breton and Pictish culture definitions are untouched.
**Religion proper**: no faiths, religions, doctrines, holy sites, holy orders, rites or great holy wars. Only tenets. No Irish saints as holy-site-linked mechanics, no Culdee/Celtic Christianity faith, no Celtic reformed pagan faith.
**Ethnicities and portraits** (hair, clothes, faces, gene data), clothes and headgear, artifact visuals, 3D models.
**Government and law**: no governments, laws, succession laws, election rules (the Irish tanistry is only simulated), vassal stances, subject contracts, tributary contracts, tax slots, court types, diarchies.
**Characters and court**: no traits (beyond fame traits), perks, lifestyles, dynasty legacies, dynasty perks, house aspirations, house unities, court positions, council positions or tasks (poet, ollamh, brehon, druid as court roles), court amenities, courtier and guest management, character backgrounds, scripted character templates (real Irish rulers as set pieces), historical bookmarks, bookmark portraits.
**Politics and war**: no new casus belli (Cattle Raid, Tribute Claim, Tanist Claim), no factions, no new schemes, no hooks (new types), no raids, no confederations, no holy orders or mercenary companies, no combat effects or phase events, no supply or travel data, no terrain types.
**Systems**: no struggles (e.g. an Irish tanistic struggle or a Norse struggle), no situations, no story cycles (the Black Host and Uprising are events, not story cycles), no epidemics (only a text reference), no great projects, no inspirations, no accolades, no legends beyond one seed, no secrets, no memories (new types), no dynasties and no houses (real Irish dynasties: Uí Néill, Eóganachta, Dál gCais), no flavorization (titles by gender and region), no domiciles.
**Interface and presentation**: no GUI (a custom window for the main story, tenets, a tracker), no event backgrounds, event themes, 2D effects, transitions, no music or sound, no scripted GUIs, no map changes, no province names or terrain edits, no new provinces (a bigger Ireland map), no custom_localization (dynamic text), no game concepts (glossary entries), no game rules, no messages, no suggestions, no tutorial.
**Historical start data**: no history/characters, history/titles, history/cultures, history/provinces besides building slots, no bookmarks, no characters for 867 or 1066.
**Vanilla events** are not touched either: no yearly events, travel, court, lifestyle, education, pregnancy, death, health, prison, scheme, faction, war, siege, secret, marriage or stress events were replaced or extended with Irish versions; they are all just new events layered on top.

## Ideas, biggest first
1. Irish activities (Óenach, Samhain/Beltane, Feis of Tara, pilgrimage, hosting).
2. Culture work: Irish pillars and innovations, name lists, eras, and the Irish Gaelic, Scottish and Brythonic cultures.
3. A Culdee / Celtic Christianity faith, a Celtic reformed pagan faith, holy sites, rites.
4. Tanistry as a real succession law with its own election rules, not a simulation.
5. Court positions: ollamh, brehon, filí, druid, hostage-keeper.
6. Casus belli: Cattle Raid, Tribute Claim, Tanist Claim.
7. A struggle: the Fragmentation of Ireland, the Norse in Ireland.
8. Story cycles for the invasions.
9. Real Irish dynasties and houses with historic characters at the 867 and 1066 starts.
10. Presentation: portraits and clothes, event backgrounds and a custom window for the main story.
