# The Great Saxon Uprising (Update 5, v0.11)

A disaster for a Gaelic ruler who holds more than 75% of England (31 of its 40 counties). It can happen **twice** per game, **50 years apart**, with a 10% chance a year once the conditions are met.

**Outbreak (event 0900).** For every duchy of England under your occupation (its holder answers to you, your realm holds half or more of its counties, and at least one county is not Celtic) a **new English leader is created**: he takes one county of the duchy in the night, becomes independent, raises about **5,000 men** (3,200 levies and 18 regiments of men-at-arms), claims the duchy and declares war. England has 10 duchies, so up to seven or eight wars can run at once. Your first answer: call the hosting, offer terms, rally the loyal lords, hire mercenaries or order the bishops to preach.

**In the middle (events 0901-0903).** *The Beacons of Wessex* (pardon, bribe, terror or garrison), *Treachery in the Rebel Camp* (bribe, forge, buy the strongest leader), *The Council at Winchester* (the leaders unite and become stronger unless you strike, parley, burn or hire). Each one changes the strength of the rebel leaders for the rest of the wars.

**Each war.** A rebel win ravages the duchy: -1 development in every county of the duchy held by you or the rebel (a third lose a second level), a 20-year Ravaged modifier, a 45% chance of changing to the rebel leader's culture, and your regional buildings downgraded a level in 60% of provinces (event 0905). A rebel loss gives the duchy a Liberated modifier (event 0904).

**The end.** When every war is settled: *The Saxons Are Crushed* (no war lost: the Saxon-Breaker nickname, a 40-year Subdued modifier, a moot, a minster), *The Rising Is Put Down, At a Price* (as many wins as losses) or *The Saxons Shatter Your Hold* (more losses: a Shaken modifier and a hard choice). The Rebuild the Ravaged Counties decision repairs the damage and leaves the counties stronger.

Data: `tools/gen_script_gsu.py`, `tools/ev_gsu.py`, `tools/v11_world.py`.
