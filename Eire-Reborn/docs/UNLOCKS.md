# How to get every unit and building (Eire Reborn v0.8)

## Men-at-arms (7)
| Unit | How to unlock it |
|---|---|
| Kern Javelineers | Open to every Gaelic culture from the start |
| Fianna Warband | Decision *Charter the Schools of the Filí*, or the Schools of the Filí tradition |
| Gallowglass | Decision *Claim the Irish Sea* (which also gives the *Kings of the Irish Sea* tradition) |
| Guard of Tara | Decision *End the Tanistic Fragmentation* or *Proclaim the Sea-Kingdom* (or the Stable High Kingship tradition) |
| Royal Ceithern | Decision *Raise the Royal Ceithern* (late game: needs the Oath of the Provincial Kings) |
| Insular Spearmen | Decision *Proclaim the Insular Union* (adds the Union of the Insular Celts tradition) |
| Norse-Bane Axemen | Break the Black Host (event), then decision *Swear the Oath of the Norse-Bane* |

## Regional buildings (24 families, tribal and feudal versions)
Tribal holdings use the 2-level tribal versions (75 gold + 200 prestige, then 100 gold + 350 prestige). Castle, city and church holdings use the 4-level feudal versions (150 / 250 / 340 / 500 gold). Open from the start: Dún, Monastic School, Holy Well Shrine, Hermitage, Pilgrim Hospice, Aonach Fair Ground, Bruidhean, Smithy of Goibniu, Hobby Stables, Booley Pastures, Fosterage Hall, Ogham Pillar Field, Hosting Ground.

Unlocked by a decision:

| Building | Unlock |
|---|---|
| Great Ringfort | Unlocked by the decision Raise the Ringforts |
| Crannóg Stronghold | Unlocked by the decision Raise the Ringforts |
| Round Tower | Unlocked by the decision Raise the Round Towers |
| High Cross | Unlocked by the decision Raise the High Crosses |
| Great Scriptorium | Unlocked by the decision Found a Great Scriptorium |
| Bardic School | Unlocked by the decision Charter the Schools of the Filí |
| Brehon Court | Unlocked by the decision Compile the Brehon Laws |
| Great Cattle Enclosure | Unlocked by the decision Raise the Ringforts or Codify the Bó-aire |
| Fianna Lodge | Unlocked by the decision Charter the Schools of the Filí |
| Irish Sea Quay | Unlocked by the decision Claim the Irish Sea |
| Longship Yard | Unlocked by the decision Claim the Irish Sea |

## Special buildings
A special building can be built in one particular barony, by a tribal or feudal holder alike, once the gate is met (gates are shown greyed-out in the build menu). The Hall of Tara now stands at Trim, because the barony called Tara in the game files is in Siberia.
